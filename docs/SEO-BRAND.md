means no indexed site or account surfaced in web search, not "available". Every

## 2. Name candidates

Rejected outright: SW Scoop, Clapham/Battersea Scoop, Junction, Southside, Insider,
Buzz, Beat, Matters, Nappy Valley, SE22, The Nine.

| Name | Style | Existing use found | Domain / handle evidence | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Common** (editions: *The Common: Battersea & Clapham*, later *Fulham*, *Dulwich*) | Family masthead | No London newsletter of this name; "common" as shared ground works across south London | No live site found at thecommon.co.uk / .london; handle unverified | **Recommended masthead.** Descriptive word, so register as logo + word. Strapline carries the places |
| **The Clapham Common** | Place pun | Park, two council brands, CCMAC owns claphamcommon.info; no media title with "The" | No live site found | Strong single-title name but excludes Battersea (Love Battersea sensitivity) and does not stretch to Fulham/Dulwich. Keep as the Clapham-edition sub-name only |
| **Southsider** / **The Southsider** | Voice/family | No London title found; used by Glasgow magazines | No live site found | Stretches across all south-of-river editions. Conceptual clash with Southside Wandsworth. Second choice |
| **Between the Commons** | Place | Estate-agent area name (Foxtons, Dexters, Hamptons) | No newsletter found | Evocative but agents own the phrase in search and it excludes Nine Elms and Balham. Use as a section name |
| **Battersea & Clapham** | Descriptive | Winkworth magazine uses it generically | None | Unregistrable, zero distinctiveness. Use as strapline, never masthead |
| **The Common Room** | Voice | Two local venues (The Northcote's private room SW11, Omnibus Theatre SW4) plus a national charity | None for media | Charming but class 41 clearance risk. Low |
| **The Rise** (Battersea Rise) or a coined word | Voice | Not exhaustively searched | None found | Coined names are the only route to a clean trademark and clean handles. Test two alongside The Common |
| Postcode family: *[Brand] SW11 · SW4 · SW6 · SE22* | Format | SE Magazines prove postcode editions work in print | | Good as URL and edition scaffold under one masthead |

**Recommendation.** Masthead **The Common**, strapline "Battersea, Clapham, Nine
Elms & Balham. Three mornings a week." Battersea first. Editions later become
"The Common: Fulham" and "The Common: Dulwich" with their own straplines. Register
`thecommon.co.uk` (primary) and `.com` (defensive) if free; `.london` only as a
redirect. If the .co.uk is taken, fall back to `thecommonlondon.co.uk` or move to
Southsider. Secure identical handles on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Threads,
WhatsApp Channel and Bluesky the same day.

**Trademark.** UKIPO online filing in classes 16 (printed matter), 41 (publishing)
and 35 (advertising services). Fees reported at roughly £205 for the first class
plus £60 per additional class from April 2026, so about £325 for three. Timeline
three to four months if unopposed (examination ~10 days, two-month opposition
window). File a stylised logo with the word to get past the descriptiveness
objection a bare "Common" would attract. Companies House name only needs to not be
"same as" an existing company; it confers no brand rights.

**TLD norms.** UK hyperlocals sit on .co.uk (Manchester Mill, SW Londoner, Southwark
News, London SE1) with .com for societies and BIDs; no London hyperlocal found on
.london. Use .co.uk.

## 3. Platform choice (SEO lens)

| Paid tier | Built in, 10% fee | Built in | Built in via Stripe | You build it |
| Cost at 5k subs | 10% of paid revenue | Free to 2,500 then Lite tier | ~$25 to $50/month | Hosting only |
| Programmatic pages | No | Limited | Possible via themes/routes | Yes |

**Recommendation.** Year one on **beehiiv** with the custom domain from day one
(zero cost, acceptable SEO, built-in referral programme and paid tier, exportable).
Build the directory, events calendar and evergreen guides on **our own Next.js site
at the same domain** (the platform in PLAN.md), with beehiiv serving `/newsletter`
or a subdomain. Move the newsletter onto Ghost or our own sender when the list
passes ~10k or beehiiv pricing bites. Never let the archive live only on a
`*.beehiiv.com` subdomain.

## 4. SEO strategy

### 4a. What the evidence says

- No UK or US local newsletter attributes its growth to search. Mill Media, London
  Centric, Edinburgh Minute and the US Scoops grew on word of mouth, referrals,
  Instagram and paid social. Treat SEO as the compounding second channel over 12 to
  24 months and as the moat (directory + events + guides) that an email-only
  competitor like SW Scoop cannot copy.
- Pan-London guides own the head terms by scale: Secret London ~1m visits/month,
  Londonist ~0.6m, both over half organic. Their Clapham pages rank for "things to
  do in Clapham" and "where to eat in Clapham".
- But the SERPs are soft where it matters. "Things to do in Clapham" is hotel
  chains, GoCity, Expedia and an estate agent. "Clapham Common events this weekend"
  is Skiddle and aggregators. "Balham brunch" has no local incumbent. "New
  restaurants Battersea" is Hot Dinners, London on the Inside and Battersea Beat's
  Instagram, all beatable on freshness with a dated tracker page.
- Google News inclusion is now automatic for sites meeting the content policies
  (manual Publisher Center submissions ended March 2025). Discover eligibility
  needs indexed pages, bylines, dates, NewsArticle markup and large images.
- Google Business Profile is not available to an online-only publisher.
- Newsletter archive pages earn little search traffic on their own; evergreen,
  well-titled pages do. Archives get descriptive titles and Article schema, but
  the SEO engine is the dedicated pages below.

### 4b. Page types, in priority order

1. **"What's on in Battersea, Clapham & Balham this weekend"**: one permanent URL,
   updated every Friday, dated sections, Event schema per listing. Targets
   "what's on Battersea this weekend", "Clapham this weekend", "Clapham Common
   events". Linked from every issue.
2. **Evergreen area guides**: Things to do in Clapham / Battersea / Nine Elms /
   Balham. Beatable SERPs. 1,500+ words, maps, updated quarterly, our photos.
3. **New openings trackers** per high street: Battersea Power Station & Nine Elms,
   Northcote Road, Clapham High Street & Old Town, Balham, Lavender Hill. Updated
   as news breaks, NewsArticle schema, dated entries. This is where scoops earn
   editorial backlinks from Hot Dinners and Time Out ("as first reported by").
4. **"Best X in Y" guides**: brunch (Clapham, Balham, Battersea), pubs (Clapham Old
   Town, Battersea), coffee (Northcote Road), kids' activities (Between the
   Commons), run clubs and gyms (Clapham Common), Sunday roasts, date nights.
5. **Landmark hubs**: Battersea Power Station (events, restaurants, parking, Lift
   109, Christmas), Clapham Common (events, Oktoberfest, bandstand), Battersea Park
   (Pump House, zoo, fireworks), Clapham Grand, Omnibus Theatre.
6. **Programmatic business directory**: one page per independent business across
   SW11/SW4/SW8/SW12 (hours, address, map, our two-line take, latest mention in the
   newsletter). Seeded from OSM and Google Places. Keep `noindex` until a page has
   unique content; thin pages hurt the whole domain.
7. **Issue archive**: descriptive titles ("Northcote Road pedestrianisation vote,
   new Nine Elms ramen, Clapham Common sauna"), Article schema, internal links to
   hubs.
8. **Explainers** on planning and transport (Clapham Junction redevelopment, Nine
   Elms Northern line, Northcote Road pedestrianisation): Discover bait and
   Wikipedia-citable.

### 4d. Backlink plan

- **BIDs**: The Junction BID (directory + blog) and This Is Clapham (~360 levy
  payers). Offer free event listings and a monthly "BID news" line in exchange for a
  directory entry and a partner link.
- **Civic societies**: Battersea Society, Clapham Society, CJAG (has a links page),
  Lavender Hill For Me, Friends of Clapham Common, CCMAC.
- **Councils**: Wandsworth press office and Brightside Online; Lambeth Focus on
  Clapham. Ask to be on the local media list for releases.
- **Battersea Power Station** press office: local media list and event listings.
- **Nextdoor** publisher integration (launched July 2025, 3,500 titles): apply once
  the domain has consistent output. Jacksonville Today found it a top-three organic
  signup source.
- **Pan-London press**: Hot Dinners, London on the Inside and Time Out credit
  originators. Break openings first and the links follow.
- **Wikipedia**: do not add yourself to External Links. Get cited as a source for
  facts you originally report on the Clapham, Battersea, Nine Elms and Balham
  articles.
- Schools, churches, Facebook groups and Reddit are distribution, not links.

### 4e. Technical checklist

- Custom domain from day one; Search Console verified; XML sitemap; canonical tags
  on every page; descriptive URLs.
- Schema: NewsArticle on issues and explainers, Event on calendar entries,
  BreadcrumbList everywhere, Organization with logo and sameAs to social handles.
  LocalBusiness only as nested data on directory pages.
- Core Web Vitals: static rendering for guides and directory, images compressed,
  no third-party scripts beyond analytics.
- Analytics without a cookie banner (Plausible or self-hosted).
- Bylines, dates, an About page with a named editor and address, a corrections
  policy: these are Google News and Discover trust signals and also what sponsors
  check.

## 5. Email deliverability for a UK sender

- Send from a subdomain (`mail.thecommon.co.uk`), never the apex.
- SPF, DKIM, and DMARC at `p=none` for the first month, then `p=quarantine`.
  Google and Yahoo's bulk-sender rules (one-click unsubscribe per RFC 8058 honoured
  within two days, spam complaints under 0.3%, aim under 0.1%) apply formally above
  5,000 messages a day but should be met from day one.
- Monitor Gmail Postmaster Tools; most SW London inboxes are Gmail, iCloud and
  Outlook, so GMX-style issues do not apply here.
- BIMI (logo in inbox) needs DMARC at quarantine/reject plus a certificate at roughly
  $700 to $1,500 a year and, for the Gmail blue tick, a registered trademark. Not
  before 10k subscribers and a registered mark.
- Warm up: 300 seed subscribers for two weeks, then grow sends gradually; double
  opt-in; prune 90-day non-openers before publishing open rates in the media kit.

## 6. Launch-week checklist for brand and SEO

1. Manual checks: registrar for thecommon.co.uk/.com, Instagram/TikTok/Threads
   handles, UKIPO search for "The Common" in classes 16/35/41, Companies House.
2. Register domains, file the trademark, open the Ltd.
3. Stand up the Next.js site with About, Contact, Privacy, Advertise, the weekend
   page and four area guides before the first issue, so the first press mentions
   land on real pages.
4. Submit the sitemap; request Nextdoor publisher access; email the two BIDs and
   three societies for links.
5. Launch issue 1; send the launch note to Press Gazette, Hold the Front Page and
   Behind Local News, which all cover new local titles.
